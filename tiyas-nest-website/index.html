Yes. Every order is delivered free to any city in Pakistan, whatever its size.

How do I pay?

Cash on delivery, bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa. If you choose a transfer, we send our account details on WhatsApp when we confirm your order.

How long does delivery take?

We share the expected delivery date when we confirm your order on WhatsApp, based on your city.

How do I stick a window feeder?

Clean the outside of the glass, wet the suction cups a little and press them firmly onto the window. Place it somewhere birds can see, away from spots where cats can jump up.

Which birds will visit?

Birds that already live around your home, such as sparrows and bulbuls, often find a new feeder once it has seeds in it. Be patient, it can take a few days for them to trust it.

Are the toys for small or big birds?

They suit small and medium pet birds like budgies, lovebirds and cockatiels. Message us with your bird's type if you are not sure.
